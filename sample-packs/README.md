# Koen Advisors Sample Packs

These folders contain sample finance data and starter workpapers for demonstrating the skills in this repository.

Born2Cycle is the fictional demo company inside the sample data. Koen Advisors is the sample-pack publisher brand used by the repo-facing folders and archives.

## Packs

| Pack | Contents | Use when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `koen-advisors-finance-data-pack/` | May 2026 CSV and markdown files covering P&L, balance sheet, cash forecast, AR/AP aging, bank transactions, sales, customers, vendors, inventory, refunds, processor settlements, and board commentary. | You need demo files for monthly financial review, cash investigation, forensic finance analysis, or board-pack review. |
| `koen-advisors-self-improving-finance-gdrive-starter/` | Google Drive-style folders for company context, monthly close evidence, controller notes, quality gates, finance process documentation, and learning templates. | You need a starter structure for demonstrating a self-improving finance close process. |

## Portable Archives

The `packages/` folder contains renamed zip archives for moving the sample packs into another workspace:

- `koen-advisors-finance-data-pack.zip`
- `koen-advisors-self-improving-finance-gdrive-starter.zip`

## Important Notes

- Treat these files as examples, not factual business memory.
- Do not use sample values as support for live client work.
- Keep the sample folders and zip archives in sync when changing the packs.
