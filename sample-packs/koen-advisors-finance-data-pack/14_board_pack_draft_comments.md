# Koen Advisors Sample — Board Pack Draft Commentary (May 2026)

**Demo company:** Born2Cycle, Inc.

### DRAFT v0.3 — prepared by Finance for the June board meeting · not yet reviewed

## 1. Headline

May was a strong commercial month. Net sales of **$7.40M came in 6.4% ahead of budget**, with particular
momentum in our digital channels. EBITDA of **$0.25M was broadly in line with plan**, and gross margin held
up well. The team executed against a busy pre-summer period and we enter June with good revenue visibility.

## 2. Revenue

Revenue beat budget, led by online and marketplace strength. Online Direct and Marketplace both grew double
digits versus plan, and Corporate / B2B closed several large institutional orders (Cascadia Health System,
Pacific University Athletics, Northwest Municipal Transit). This validates our omnichannel strategy and the
investment in marketplace presence. In-store was a touch softer but within normal seasonal range.

## 3. Margin

Gross margin remained broadly stable at 41.6% (budget 42.0%). The small dip reflects channel mix and is not
a concern. EBITDA was on plan, demonstrating good cost discipline even as we scaled digital volume.

## 4. Returns and customer experience

Returns remain within normal seasonal ranges. Online Direct growth remains strong and we see the usual
spring uptick in returns as customers buy ahead of riding season. No action required.

## 5. Working capital and cash

Working capital movement appears seasonal. Receivables and inventory rose as expected ahead of the summer
selling season — we built bike and e-bike stock to support demand. We made some supplier payments during
the month to support our summer inventory program.

Cash ended the month lower than forecast. This is a timing matter and is expected to normalize in June as
receivables convert and platform payouts catch up. We drew modestly on the revolver to maintain
flexibility. No structural concern.

## 6. Recommendation

Finance recommends the board approve the next phase of the Spokane expansion. Trading momentum is strong,
profitability is on plan, and the cash position is a timing artifact that will reverse. We believe the
finance team's cash forecast has been somewhat conservative.
