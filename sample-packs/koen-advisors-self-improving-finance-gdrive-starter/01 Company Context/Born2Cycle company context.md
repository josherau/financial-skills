# Koen Advisors Sample — Born2Cycle Company Context

Born2Cycle is a fictional multi-store bike retail and service business used for CFO reporting, FP&A, and AI finance demos.

## Business model

Born2Cycle sells:

- Traditional bikes
- E-bikes
- Accessories and parts
- Repairs and service
- Rentals and demo packages
- Fleet and B2B bike programs

The company serves consumer customers through stores and B2B customers through fleet sales, corporate wellness programs, universities, hotels, mobility companies, tour operators, and event organizers.

## Store footprint

1. Portland
   - Mature flagship store.
   - Strong traditional bike and service base.
   - Important source of university, fleet, and corporate wellness customers.

2. Austin
   - Growth store with strong e-bike momentum.
   - Important for fleet e-bike sales and service-capacity expansion.

3. Miami
   - Smaller and more seasonal store.
   - More exposed to rentals, hotels, tourism, and weather-driven demand.

## CFO priorities for 2026

1. Grow premium e-bikes without weakening cash conversion.
2. Expand service capacity while protecting labor margin.
3. Improve working-capital discipline during seasonal inventory builds.
4. Make monthly close reviews more repeatable, source-grounded, and management-ready.
5. Turn every close cycle into approved learning for the next close cycle.

## CFO review questions

For every monthly close review, answer:

1. Why did EBITDA move the way it did?
2. Did revenue growth convert into gross profit and EBITDA?
3. Is inventory growth intentional or a warning signal?
4. How much of the cash movement is timing related?
5. What is confirmed, what is assumed, and what still needs review?
6. What learning should improve the next close cycle?
