# Koen Advisors Sample — April 2026 Close Notes

**Demo company:** Born2Cycle

Raw notes from Maya Patel, Controller support.

## Known issues before review

- April revenue beat budget again, driven by Austin e-bike fleet sales and stronger Portland service revenue.
- EBITDA improved versus March, but conversion is still below the CFO's expectation because e-bike gross margin remains under pressure.
- Inventory increased again, but the reason is mixed. Some growth is planned spring stocking. Some growth may be slow-moving e-bike inventory from the March shipment.
- The CFO specifically asked us to use the March approved learning: compare inventory growth against revenue, gross margin, and known seasonal stocking decisions before calling it intentional.
- Cash improved less than expected because supplier payments and a partial revolver repayment offset operating receipts.
- AR includes a large Austin Mobility balance. Collections are current but concentrated.
- Marketing rebounded in April because the spring campaign shifted from March to April. Do not treat the April marketing increase as a new run-rate without checking campaign timing.
- Service labor overtime declined, but still needs review because Portland service revenue increased faster than planned.
- There is a $95k vendor invoice from BatteryWorks that may relate to March receipts but was posted in April.
- The CFO wants a short management-ready close review, not a detailed accounting memo.

## CFO questions

1. Did April revenue growth convert better than March?
2. Is the inventory build intentional or becoming a warning signal?
3. How much of the cash movement is timing related?
4. Which March learning items were applied in the April review?
5. What new learning candidates should improve the May close?
