# 2026-03 March Close Review Memo

## Executive summary

March revenue beat budget, but EBITDA did not improve as much as the revenue upside would suggest. The main issue was conversion. E-bike fleet sales helped revenue, but battery supplier costs, service overtime, and interest expense absorbed part of the upside.

Inventory increased materially. Part of the build appears intentional because Born2Cycle is preparing for spring e-bike and service-parts demand. However, the inventory review was not clean enough because in-transit items, service parts, and a late receiving cut-off issue made it difficult to separate planned stocking from timing noise.

Cash declined despite the revenue beat. The main drivers were vendor payments, payroll, tax payments, and inventory funding. The revolver draw supported the inventory build.

## CFO messages for management

1. Revenue momentum is healthy, especially in e-bikes, but margin conversion needs attention.
2. Inventory should be reviewed with more discipline before calling it strategic.
3. Cash movement needs a simple bridge so timing and operating performance are not mixed together.

## Open issues

| Issue | Why it matters | Follow-up |
|---|---|---|
| Inventory build | Impacts cash and revolver usage | Compare inventory growth to revenue, gross margin, and seasonal stocking plan |
| Battery supplier cost | Pressures e-bike margin | Confirm whether cost increase is temporary or structural |
